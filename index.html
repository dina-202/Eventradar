ABOUT EVENTRADAR

A focused event discovery experience.

EventRadar is designed to make browsing UK events quick and uncomplicated. The project is intentionally lightweight: discover events, narrow the results and continue to the official event source.

It does not sell tickets, process payments or provide a competing ticketing service. Once connected, Skiddle will be credited as the event data source and users will be directed to Skiddle for the relevant event listing.
