Privacy at EventRadar

Last updated: 6 October 2026

About this demo

EventRadar is an independent, non-commercial UK event-discovery demo. The current version uses sample event listings and does not require user accounts.

Information we collect

The demo itself does not ask for names, email addresses, payment details or other personal information. Search and filter selections are handled in your browser and are not stored by EventRadar.

External event links

After Skiddle API access is approved, EventRadar is intended to show event information supplied by Skiddle and provide links to the corresponding event pages on Skiddle. External sites have their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes

This page may be updated if the project later adds features that change how information is handled.
